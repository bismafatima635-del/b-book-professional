Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how B-Book collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our platform. We are committed to respecting your privacy and handling personal information responsibly. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices and choices.

1. About B-Book

B-Book is a business-focused social platform being developed to help businesses and users discover businesses, share content, and connect. The initial launch is planned for Karachi, Pakistan.

For privacy questions, contact bbook0859@gmail.com.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features made available and how you use the app, B-Book may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: details such as your name, username, email address, and authentication information.
  • Profile and business information: profile photos, business name, category, description, contact details, and information you choose to publish.
  • User content: photos, videos, stories, reels, comments, and other content you upload or share.
  • Communications: information necessary to provide messaging or other communication features, if available.
  • Location information: location details you choose to provide or permit the app to access for relevant features.
  • Technical and usage information: device information, app diagnostics, security logs, and usage data, where collected.
  • Support messages: information you provide when contacting us for help.

Only information actually collected by the released app should be described as collected. The final policy must be updated to reflect the implemented features and permissions.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Create and manage accounts and business profiles.
  • Display content and provide search or discovery features.
  • Enable available social and communication features.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect users, enforce platform rules, and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
  • Display advertising or paid business promotions, where enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Content you publish publicly, such as business profiles, posts, comments, photos, or videos, may be visible to other users. Do not share information you do not want others to see. Review the audience and visibility settings provided by the app, where available.
