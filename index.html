Meet B-Book

A digital space built around people and businesses.

Finding a business, discovering a new brand, or sharing what you create should feel natural. B-Book aims to bring business discovery and social interaction into one accessible experience, giving businesses and people more opportunities to find each other.

Our vision

Make every business easier to discover.

From independent shops and cafés to service providers and growing brands, every business has something worth sharing. B-Book is being built to give those stories a digital home and help people explore the businesses around them.

Our starting focus is Karachi, Pakistan. The broader vision is to develop a welcoming platform where business presence, community interaction, and discovery can work together.

For business owners

Your business deserves a digital presence.

A strong online presence helps people understand what you offer before they contact you or visit your shop. B-Book aims to make business presentation and discovery approachable for both established brands and people starting something new.

01

Introduce your brand

Present your business identity and help people understand your products, services, values, and what makes your offering distinctive.

02

Showcase your work

Use engaging content to tell your story, introduce new products, share useful updates, and communicate the personality behind your brand.

03

Build connections

Create opportunities to connect with interested people, discover other businesses, and develop relationships within a wider business community.

Business reach, customer growth, and promotional results are not guaranteed. Availability of specific tools depends on the app's actual release and functionality.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Learn more about B-Book, its purpose, and the direction of the platform.

What is B-Book?

B-Book is being developed as a business-focused social networking platform intended to help businesses present their brands, share content, and connect with people through a digital community.

Who is B-Book designed for?

The intended audience includes business owners, entrepreneurs, local service providers, brands, and people interested in discovering businesses and engaging with relevant content.

Where will B-Book launch first?

The initial launch focus is Karachi, Pakistan. Any expansion to additional cities or regions will depend on the platform's development and future plans.

Will businesses be able to promote themselves?

Paid business promotions are a planned potential offering. Their availability, pricing, eligibility, and terms must be confirmed before businesses can purchase a promotion.

Will B-Book be free to use?

The goal is to make getting started accessible. Any paid promotions or other paid services will need to be clearly identified. Specific features, charges, and availability will be explained when confirmed.
