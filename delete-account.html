Account Deletion

You can contact B-Book to request deletion of your account and associated personal data. We will need to verify that you own the account before processing a request.

How to request account deletion

  1. Click the “Request Account Deletion” button below.
  2. Your email application will open with a deletion-request subject.
  3. Include your B-Book username and the email address associated with your account, if applicable.
  4. Send the email. We may contact you if additional information is needed to verify your request.

What happens after you request deletion?

We will review your request and may ask you to verify account ownership. If the request is valid, account deletion and removal of associated personal data will be handled according to the app's actual deletion process and applicable law.
